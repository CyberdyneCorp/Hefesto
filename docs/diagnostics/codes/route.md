# Diagnostic codes: route area

Codes `ROUTE-<TOPIC>-<NNN>` emitted by `src/core/src/route/` (the maze
router: the technology `mzrouter` section and styles, the maze engine,
`iroute` and the `*iroute` / `*mzroute` diagnostics; capabilities
router-maze and router-maze-iroute; `openspec/changes/m10-plot-route/design.md`
D3). Conventions and the renderings are described in
[../codes.md](../codes.md).

The area's table is assembled by `src/core/src/route/codes.cpp` (owner:
SCAFFOLD) from one fragment per workstream, each owned by that workstream.
The rows are sorted while compiling (`src/core/src/diag/sorted_codes.hpp`):

| Fragment | Topics | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| `src/core/src/route/tech/codes.inc` | `ROUTE-TECH-*` | MZTECH |
| `src/core/src/route/maze/codes.inc` | `ROUTE-MZ-*` | MZ |
| `src/core/src/route/iroute/codes.inc` | `ROUTE-IROUTE-*` | IROUTE |
| `src/core/src/route/diag/codes.inc` | `ROUTE-DIAG-*` | IROUTE |

The scaffold (tasks.md 3.1) declared no code. Until a workstream lands its
part, `iroute`, `*iroute`, `*mzroute` and the area-contract functions
report `CORE-NOTIMPL-010` ([../codes.md](../codes.md)).

## MZTECH

Codes of the technology `mzrouter` section reader, the style derivation
from DRC and the derived `irouter` style (`route/tech/`; "mzrouter section
grammar", "Style defaults and lookup", "Width and spacing derivation from
DRC", "Derived irouter style"). None yet.

## MZ

Codes of the maze engine (`route/maze/`; the other router-maze
requirements). None yet.

## IROUTE

Codes of `iroute`, its parameters, `saveParameters` and endpoints
(`route/iroute/`), and of the `*iroute` and `*mzroute` diagnostics
(`route/diag/`; the router-maze-iroute requirements). None yet.
